The rule

A shortage is active when any one of three signals is on:

  • Price: the good's 6-month price rise is at least 1.5 standard deviations above its own 10-year history and the price is at a 24-month high. It stays on until the price falls below its 6-month average, or 24 months pass. 30 goods are tracked.
  • News: posts pairing the industry with "shortage" reach twice their own 2-year rate, with at least five mentions in three months and shortage talk more unusual than glut talk. It stays on until mentions fall back below normal.
  • Earnings calls: at least three S&P 500 companies cite a shortage of the same input in three months, at twice the 2-year norm.

Every 21 trading days, rank the active shortages by the 6-month return of their producer basket and hold the top three, one third each. A holding whose shortage ends is sold at once. Idle money sits in T-bills.

Every signal is traded only after its data was public: 5 to 15 trading days into the following month for prices, day 2 for news and calls.

Earnings-call text after May 2025

The original call data ended on 15 May 2025. A second open dataset extends it to 12 May 2026 with 2,529 calls from the same S&P 500 companies. Where the two overlap (764 calls), their shortage and glut sentence counts correlate at 0.98, so they were joined into one series.

  • New call-based shortage signals: electric power and grid connection (November 2025), memory chips and mature-node chips (February 2026), aluminium, crude oil and housing (April 2026).
  • Effect on the rotation: almost none. Return moved from 39.9% to {{CAGR}} a year, because most of those themes were already flagged by price or news, or did not rank in the top three.
  • There is still no call text after May 2026. From then on only price and news feed the signal.
  • The second dataset was collected by a third party from a transcript website and carries a non-standard licence. Only derived counts are stored, not the text.

What to keep in mind

  • The rule was chosen after the fact. Monthly top-three was the best of the variants tried on this same data. Expect less going forward.
  • Gains are concentrated. Ten trades made {{TOP10}} of the profit. The memory-chip trade alone is the largest single contributor.
  • It is a rough ride. Volatility is {{VOL}} a year with a {{DD}} drawdown, against {{VOL_SPY}} and {{DD_SPY}} for SPY. It lagged SPY by 29 points in 2019 and 12 in 2023.
  • One period. 2018 to 2026 contains one large shortage cluster (2020–22) and one AI hardware boom.
  • The news source is one technology forum with small counts, and the theme baskets were written in 2026 with hindsight. Currency moves on non-US tickers and futures roll costs on the soft commodities are not modelled.
